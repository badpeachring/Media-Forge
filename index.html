How To Edit Clothes / Objects Cleanly

1. Highlight without tracing: In Photo Editor, tap 🪄 Tap (Wand) and tap directly on the shirt. Tap shaded folds once more, or use ⭕ Loop to circle it.

2. Describe what REPLACES it: Do NOT type "take white shirt off" in the main prompt—that tells the AI to draw a white shirt! Instead, describe what should appear inside the highlight (e.g., "red bikini top, natural skin, matching anime art style") and put "white shirt, top" in the Negative Prompt.
