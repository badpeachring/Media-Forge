Media Forge Quick Guide

Text-to-Image vs Photo-to-Image: Text mode generates from scratch. Photo mode uploads your picture, shrinks it cleanly, and lets the AI restyle it based on Change Strength (Denoising).

Uncensored Mode: Sends nsfw:true and censor_nsfw:false directly to volunteer GPUs that do not block or blur adult/edgy prompts.

Queue Wait Times: Anonymous runs are 100% free but wait behind registered accounts. Pick models with high GPU counts and keep size at 512x512 or 768x768 for 10-30s renders.

Lock Seed & Remix: Lock Seed keeps the exact visual layout while you tweak words. Remix loads the finished picture right back into Photo-to-Image.
